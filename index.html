What does a Creative Director do?

A Creative Director finds the brand's big idea and turns it into an ad, a campaign, a discount, a program, a piece of content. I stay with a brand from the first spark of an idea to launch and beyond — ideation for advertising, campaigns, discounts, programs, content and much more.
